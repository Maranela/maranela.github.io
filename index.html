📄 Privacy Notice - How Your Data Is Handled

This app is built to protect you, not collect your data.

1. Where data is stored: Everything (your name, username, contacts, evidence videos) is stored ONLY on your own phone using localStorage. We have NO external server, NO database, NO cloud.

2. ID handling: We do NOT store your full SA ID. We show you live validation, then we keep only the last 4 digits (e.g....7777) and a hashed version for verification. Full ID is never saved on any server.

3. Location: Your location is accessed ONLY when YOU press SOS or start Safe Trip. It is sent directly to your trusted contacts via SMS/WhatsApp you choose. It is not sent to us or any third party.

4. Evidence Vault: Video/audio recordings are saved locally on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere unless YOU choose to share them.

5. Permissions: Camera/mic = to record evidence if you trigger SOS. Location = to share rescue location. SMS = to send SOS to your contacts.

6. Your rights: You can delete all data anytime: Log Out + Clear App Data, or uninstall the app. All data is removed from your device.

7. POPIA Note: We aim to follow POPIA principles of minimal collection and local storage. This notice explains our actual practice. We do not claim formal POPIA certification or registration with the Information Regulator - we simply do not collect your data on a server.
